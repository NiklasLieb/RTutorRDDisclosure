FIRM_ID | FIRM_ID | Anonymized firm identifier. Labels each company in the sample and stays constant over time.

POST | POST | Dummy equal to one for firm-year observations in the post-event period of the event, and zero for the pre-event period.

TREATED_X_POST | TREATED_X_POST | Interaction of TREATED and POST.
